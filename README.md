# Loan Amount Regression Analysis

## Overview

This project applies statistical and regression analysis to examine the factors associated with loan amounts.

The analysis investigates relationships between loan amount and variables such as applicant income, co-applicant income, total income, loan term, credit history, and other borrower characteristics.

The project uses descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, regression modelling, and data-driven interpretation to understand the relationships within the dataset.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Examine the distribution and characteristics of key financial variables.
- Analyze relationships between income and loan amount.
- Investigate the relationship between loan term and loan amount.
- Examine correlations between selected numerical variables.
- Apply regression analysis to quantify relationships between variables.
- Develop derived financial indicators for further analysis.
- Interpret statistical results from a business perspective.
- Generate data-driven insights and recommendations.

---

# Methodology

The analysis followed a structured statistical workflow:

### 1. Data Preparation

The loan dataset was reviewed and prepared for statistical analysis.

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics were calculated for key numerical variables, including:

- Applicant Income
- Co-Applicant Income
- Total Income
- Loan Amount
- Loan Amount Term

Measures such as mean and standard error were examined to understand the characteristics of the variables.

### 3. Correlation Analysis

Correlation matrices were used to investigate relationships between numerical variables in both the training and test datasets.

### 4. Regression Analysis

Regression models were developed to investigate relationships involving Loan Amount.

The analysis includes:

- Loan Amount vs. Loan Amount Term
- Loan Amount vs. Total Income

### 5. Derived Variables

Additional analytical variables were incorporated into the dataset, including:

- Total Income
- Loan-to-Income Ratio
- EMI Estimate
- High Income classification
- High Loan classification

### 6. Insights and Recommendations

The statistical results were interpreted and translated into practical insights and recommendations.

---

# Regression Analysis

## Model 1: Loan Amount vs. Loan Amount Term

This regression examines the relationship between the requested loan amount and the loan repayment term.

The objective is to determine whether variation in loan amount is meaningfully associated with loan duration.

## Model 2: Loan Amount vs. Total Income

This regression examines the relationship between total applicant income and loan amount.

Total income combines applicant and co-applicant income and provides a broader measure of the applicant's financial capacity.
